Kept Memories Privacy Policy

Effective September 29, 2026.

Kept Memories (“Kept”) is an Android app that builds memories from the photos and videos on your phone. In short: your photos are analyzed only on your device and are never uploaded. The only thing that goes online is the approximate location of a place, to look up the name of the city.

Developer: [developer name]. Contact: [contact e-mail].

What the app reads

  • Photos and videos in your phone’s media library and their properties: date taken, size, folder, favorite mark, EXIF geotag. You grant access in the system dialog; with partial access Kept sees only the photos you selected.
  • Kept only reads your library: it never deletes, moves or changes your files. The only thing the app writes to your gallery is a collage you save yourself with “Save to gallery” (the Pictures/Kept folder).

What is computed on your device

All of the following is computed on the phone and stored in the app’s private storage, which other apps cannot access:

  • frame quality (sharpness, exposure) and duplicate detection;
  • whether a photo has faces, where they are, smiles and open eyes;
  • grouping faces into people (the “Recognize people” setting, on by default): a built-in SFace model computes a numeric fingerprint of each face (128 numbers), and similar faces are put into groups — that is how memories like “A year in faces” come about. The fingerprints and groups never leave the phone, are not backed up and are not matched against anything outside: Kept does not know who these people are unless you give a person a name yourself. Turning the setting off deletes all fingerprints, groups and names at once;
  • content labels (for example “beach”, “food”) by the Google ML Kit model built into the app, without contacting any server;
  • events, trips and places where you often take photos (for example home and work), from dates and geotags;
  • memory selections and your reactions to them.

Settings are also kept only on the phone: notification time, memory types, birthdays (a name and a date you enter yourself), people's names (also only the ones you give), hidden people, hidden dates and folders.

What goes online

Only a request for the city name of an event:

  • what is sent: the coordinates of the event’s center rounded to about a kilometer (two decimal places) and the interface language. No photos, thumbnails, dates, analysis results, and no user or device identifiers;
  • to whom:
    1. the Android system geocoder, a service built into the phone (on devices with Google services the request is handled by Google);
    2. if the system geocoder is missing or does not answer, the Nominatim service of the OpenStreetMap Foundation (nominatim.openstreetmap.org). As its usage policy requires, the request carries the app name and version (Kept/1.0 (Android; ru.nightwelf.kept)); like any web server, it sees the request’s IP address. Its policy: https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy;
  • how often: one request per place. The answer is stored on the phone for good and never requested again.

Without internet everything else works; a memory simply comes without a city name.

The usage statistics that the ML Kit library sends to Google by default are turned off in Kept.

What Kept does not do

  • It does not upload photos, videos or thumbnails anywhere.
  • It does not collect analytics or usage statistics. If that ever changes, it will be opt-in only, off by default, and this policy will be updated beforehand.
  • No ads, no account, no sharing or selling of data to third parties.
  • The developer has no servers your data could end up on.
  • Kept’s data is excluded from Android backup and device transfer: on a new phone the app sorts the gallery again.

Actions you take

  • Share hands the collage to the app you pick (a messenger, e-mail). From then on it is handled under that app’s rules.
  • Open in gallery opens the photo in your gallery app.
  • The morning notification is scheduled and shown by the phone itself; you can change its time, or turn notifications off in Android settings.

Permissions

  • Photos and videos (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES, READ_MEDIA_VIDEO, READ_MEDIA_VISUAL_USER_SELECTED, on Android 10–12 READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE): reading the library.
  • Photo locations (ACCESS_MEDIA_LOCATION): trips, cities and places. Without it Kept works without location features.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): one morning notification about a memory.
  • Internet (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): only the city name request.
  • Background work (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED, WAKE_LOCK, FOREGROUND_SERVICE): processing the gallery in small portions, mostly while charging, and restoring the schedule after a reboot.

Deleting data

You can hide a memory, a date or a photo in the app. All of Kept’s data is deleted when you uninstall the app or tap “Clear storage” in Android settings. Your photos are not affected.

Children

Kept is not directed at children and collects no data about anyone.
